# Remix Kit: A Networking Follow-Up List

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Turn an event attendee list or a pile of LinkedIn chats into a sorted follow-up list with notes, next steps and replies ready to copy.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | About 1 hour |
| **Skill level** | Beginner friendly. No coding. |
| **You end up with** | A follow-up list sorted by urgency, with a note, a next step and a drafted message for each person |

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: freelancers, salespeople, founders, job seekers and event organizers.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I just went to a networking event, I have 40 LinkedIn messages to answer, I host meetups)
2. **Where are the contacts coming from?** An event list, LinkedIn, business cards, a spreadsheet
3. **What's the goal?** Book calls, find referral partners, sell a service, stay in touch
4. **Do you use a contact list tool (a CRM)?** HubSpot, a spreadsheet, nothing yet
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

---

## Step 2: Your tech stack

A "tech stack" is just the list of tools a project uses.

| Tool | Needed? | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Yes | Sorts contacts, writes notes and drafts messages |
| [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com) or a spreadsheet | Optional | Where contacts and follow-up tasks are saved |
| [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com) | Optional | Where many of the conversations happen |

**What's a CRM?** A contact list tool that keeps notes and reminders for each person, like HubSpot.

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] The list of people (names, companies, where you met)
- [ ] Any notes you took
- [ ] Your booking link or offer
- [ ] 2 or 3 messages you've sent that sound like you

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- People sorted into **reply now, meetings to book and referral partners**
- **A note and a next step** for each person
- **Drafted messages** with a copy button
- A **progress bar** that fills as you tick people off, and a list of gaps to fill

**Why it helps:** Warm leads go cold fast after an event if nobody follows up.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Share your list
Upload or paste the contacts and notes.

### 5.2 Sort them
Claude groups people by urgency and goal. Move anyone who's in the wrong group.

### 5.3 Write notes and next steps
Claude adds one line per person and a follow-up task.

### 5.4 Draft messages
Claude writes a short message per person in your voice.

### 5.5 Save to your CRM
If connected, Claude adds each contact and task. If not, it builds a sheet.

### 5.6 Work the list
Copy, send, tick off.

---

## Make it even better

- Set a **reminder** two weeks out for anyone who didn't reply.
- Flag **missing info** like LinkedIn links to fill in later.
- Tag contacts by **event** so you can see which events pay off.

## If something goes wrong

- **Messages feel generic:** Give Claude one detail per person from your notes.
- **Contacts didn't save:** Check the CRM connection, or ask for a spreadsheet to import.
