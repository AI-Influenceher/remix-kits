## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **A weekly rhythm** of what to do on which day
- **Practical tips** tied to the client's own world (local, their team, their events)
- **Ways to reuse** what already works
- **A checklist** to tick off

**Why it helps:** People forget most of a long call. One page they can pin up sticks.
