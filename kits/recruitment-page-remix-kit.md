# Remix Kit: A Page That Gets People to Say Yes to Leading

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Write and build a page that convinces people to start or lead something, like a chapter, club or local group, with a clear first step.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | About 1 to 2 hours |
| **Skill level** | Beginner friendly. No coding. |
| **You end up with** | A ready-to-paste web page with reasons to say yes, what they get and simple steps to start |

---

## How to use this file

1. Download this file.
2. Open a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and attach it.
3. Type: **"Let's start this remix kit."**
4. Claude will walk you through it one step at a time. Answer its questions, then keep going.

You don't need to read the rest unless you're curious. Claude will handle it.

---

## Instructions for Claude (read this first)

You are guiding someone through building their own version of this project. Follow these rules for the whole conversation:

- Go one step at a time. Finish a step, confirm it with the person, then move to the next.
- Ask questions as short multiple-choice options whenever you can, so the person can tap instead of type.
- Assume the person may be new to tech. Explain any technical word in a few plain words the first time you use it.
- Use only what the person tells you, shows you or what you can check. Do not invent names, numbers, quotes or results.
- At the end of each step, say in one line what's done and what's next.

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: nonprofits, clubs, franchises, ambassador programs and community brands.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: We're looking for chapter leaders, we want brand ambassadors, we're starting city groups)
2. **What are you asking people to lead or start?**
3. **What do leaders get?** Support, tools, visibility, connections
4. **What's the very first step?** A form, a call, an email
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **hook up top** that sells the idea in one image or line
- **Short reasons to lead**, in plain words
- A list of **what leaders get**
- **Simple steps** from first contact to first event, and a sign-up button

**Why it helps:** People need a reason to say yes and an easy first step, or they stay curious and never act.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Find the hook
Claude suggests three big ideas for the top of the page. Pick one.

### 5.2 Write the reasons
Claude drafts 5 to 7 short reasons. Cut any that don't sound true.

### 5.3 List what they get and the steps
Give the facts. Claude writes them clearly.

### 5.4 Build the page
Claude builds it in your colors, ready to paste into your site builder.

### 5.5 Add it to your site
Claude gives exact steps for your website tool.
