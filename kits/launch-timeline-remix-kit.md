Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: freelancers, agencies, event planners, authors, founders and anyone planning a launch.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I'm launching a book, I'm planning a product launch, I'm running a client project)
2. **What's the launch date?**
3. **What are the projects?** Website, social, content, ads, event
4. **What days are off-limits?** Holidays, breaks, vacations
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **week-by-week chart**, with each project on its own row and work, feedback and client dates in different colors
- **Days off shaded** so no deadline lands on them
- **Payments by month**: deposits, balances and due dates
- **A client to-do list** with the day each item is needed, plus a printable plan

**Why it helps:** When dates and payments live in separate quotes, cash flow surprises happen.
