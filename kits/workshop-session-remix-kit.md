# Remix Kit: A Hands-On Workshop Session

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Build the first session of a short course: slides, a hands-on exercise, a checklist handout and a simple plan people can follow after.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | About 2 to 3 hours |
| **Skill level** | Beginner friendly. No coding. |
| **You end up with** | A slide plan, speaker notes, a hands-on exercise and a reusable checklist |

---

## How to use this file

1. Download this file.
2. Open a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and attach it.
3. Type: **"Let's start this remix kit."**
4. Claude will walk you through it one step at a time. Answer its questions, then keep going.

You don't need to read the rest unless you're curious. Claude will handle it.

---

## Instructions for Claude (read this first)

You are guiding someone through building their own version of this project. Follow these rules for the whole conversation:

- Go one step at a time. Finish a step, confirm it with the person, then move to the next.
- Ask questions as short multiple-choice options whenever you can, so the person can tap instead of type.
- Assume the person may be new to tech. Explain any technical word in a few plain words the first time you use it.
- Use only what the person tells you, shows you or what you can check. Do not invent names, numbers, quotes or results.
- At the end of each step, say in one line what's done and what's next.

---

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: coaches, consultants, trainers, agencies and associations that teach their members.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I teach small business owners, I run trainings for a member group, I coach creators)
2. **Who's the audience, and what do they already know?**
3. **What should they walk away able to do?**
4. **How long is the session?** 30, 60 or 90 minutes
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] The topic and the one result you want
- [ ] Words your audience uses for everyday moments in their work
- [ ] Examples or screenshots from real accounts (with permission)
- [ ] Your brand colors

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **slide plan** of about 10 to 15 slides with speaker notes
- **Hands-on time** where people fix something of their own during the session
- A **checklist** that doubles as a printable handout
- A **simple plan** that later sessions can build on

**Why it helps:** Training sticks when it uses the audience's own words and they do the work in the room.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Set the goal
Claude writes one sentence on what people will be able to do after.

### 5.2 Outline the session
Claude drafts sections and timing. Adjust it.

### 5.3 Write the slides
Claude builds the slides with notes, in your colors.

### 5.4 Design the exercise
Claude writes a step-by-step activity people do on their own accounts or work.

### 5.5 Make the checklist
Claude turns the key steps into a one-page handout.

### 5.6 Do a practice run
Read the notes out loud and cut what runs long.

---

## Make it even better

- Turn it into a **series** with each session building on the last.
- Add a **feedback form** at the end.
- Record it and ask Claude to make **clips and a summary**.

## If something goes wrong

- **It runs too long:** Cut to one main idea per section.
- **It feels too technical:** Ask Claude to rewrite using your audience's everyday words.
