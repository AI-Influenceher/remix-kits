## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: nonprofits, small businesses, associations and agencies.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: Our website feels outdated, we have several sites that don't match, I need a plan for my board)
2. **Which site or sites?**
3. **Who are the main visitors?** Members, buyers, donors, press
4. **How much time and help do you have?** Staff, volunteers, a developer
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **A review from several points of view**, like a designer, a search specialist, a marketer and your typical visitor
- **A menu proposal** and notes on where the design drifts from your brand
- **A phased plan** over a few months, easy fixes first
- **A decisions list** of choices that need an owner before work starts

**Why it helps:** Without a plan, website updates stall or happen piecemeal.
