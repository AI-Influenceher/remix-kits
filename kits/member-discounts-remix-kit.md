## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **A form** partners fill in
- **One-cell approval**: flip a cell to Yes and the offer appears
- **A copy-code button** on every offer
- **Auto archive**: ended offers move to past offers on their own

**Why it helps:** A perks page nobody has to edit by hand stays current.
