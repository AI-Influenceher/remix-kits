## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: small businesses, local services, clinics, agencies and marketers.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I run Google Ads for my business, I manage ads for clients, I'm not sure my ads work)
2. **What counts as a real lead for you?** A call, a form, a booking, a purchase
3. **What options are you weighing?** (A new agency, a new budget, pausing, something else)
4. **What's the date range to review?**
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

| Tool | Needed? | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Yes | Reviews the data and writes the report |

| [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com) | Optional | To check what people did after clicking |

**Common trap:** some accounts count page views as results, which makes ads look better than they are. This kit separates real contact actions.

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Headline numbers** on where the budget went
- **Searches sorted** into right customer, wrong customer and your own name
- **Each option weighed** with a verdict like Recommend, Later or Small test
- **A numbered plan** in order, plus how the same budget could be split

**Why it helps:** If page views count as leads, you can't tell if ads are paying off.
