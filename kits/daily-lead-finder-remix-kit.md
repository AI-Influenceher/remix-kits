## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: freelancers, agencies, consultants, contractors and service businesses.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I run a small agency, I freelance in design, I bid on government work)
2. **What services should leads match?**
3. **Where do good leads show up?** Public bid sites, RFP emails, LinkedIn or Reddit posts, local groups
4. **Where should the team hear about new ones?** Slack, email, or just the sheet
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

---

## Step 2: Your tech stack

A "tech stack" is just the list of tools a project uses.

| Tool | Needed? | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Yes | Searches, scores leads and writes draft openers |
| [Google Sheets](https://sheets.google.com) | Yes | The running list of leads |
| [Slack](https://slack.com) | Optional | Posts the best leads to your team the same day |
| [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov) and city or state bid portals | Optional | Public places where government requests are posted |

**What's an RFP?** A "request for proposals" is a public post where an organization asks businesses to bid on a project.

---

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] A short list of your services and the kinds of clients you want
- [ ] Places you already check for leads
- [ ] Deal breakers (too small, wrong location, needs a license you don't have)
- [ ] A sentence or two about what makes you a good fit

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **daily search** of the places you named, each weekday
- A **fit score** from 1 to 10 for each lead, with red flags noted
- **One sheet** with deadline, contact, why it fits and a draft opening line, with no duplicates
- An **alert** for the best leads in Slack or email

**Why it helps:** Finding leads shouldn't depend on someone remembering to look.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Define a good lead
Claude turns your services and deal breakers into a short scoring guide.

### 5.2 Pick your sources
Choose the sites and inboxes to check. Start with 3 to 5.

### 5.3 Set up the sheet
Claude creates columns: lead, source, deadline, fit score, why it fits, draft opener, status.

### 5.4 Run it once together
Claude searches, scores and fills the sheet. You check the scores and adjust the guide.

### 5.5 Add the alert
Connect Slack or email so top leads get posted the same day.

### 5.6 Schedule it
Claude sets a scheduled task to run every weekday morning.

## Make it even better

- Add a **"draft please" column** so Claude starts a proposal when you tick it.
- Track **won and lost** leads to sharpen the scoring over time.
- Add a **weekly summary** of new leads and upcoming deadlines.

## If something goes wrong

- **Too many weak leads:** Raise the minimum fit score or add more deal breakers.
- **Duplicate rows:** Ask Claude to check the sheet for the same title or link before adding.
