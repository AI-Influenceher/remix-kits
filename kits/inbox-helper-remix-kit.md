## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: business owners, team leads, assistants, nonprofit staff and anyone with a shared inbox.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I run my own inbox, I watch a shared info@ inbox, I inherited a messy inbox)
2. **One inbox or several?** (For example a personal inbox plus a shared one)
3. **Who else can reply?** Just you, or a team
4. **What clogs your inbox most?** Newsletters, receipts, notices, vendor pitches
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- Emails sorted into **Today, This week and Cruise control**, each with a short summary and next step
- **Real people first**: automated mail is set aside so human messages are easy to spot
- **An owner and a reply-by date** for each email, with a button that opens it
- **Ready-to-paste filters** to quiet the inbox going forward

**Why it helps:** When most of the inbox is automated, the few emails from real people get buried.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Connect the inbox
Connect Gmail to Claude in settings.

### 5.2 Set the rules
Tell Claude who owns what and what always matters.

### 5.3 Sort a first batch
Claude reads recent mail, sets aside automated messages and ranks the rest.

### 5.4 Build the page
Claude builds the sorted page with summaries, owners and open buttons.

### 5.5 Draft replies
Open a card and ask Claude for a reply. It saves as a Gmail draft.

### 5.6 Add filters
Claude writes Gmail filters you paste in to keep the noise out.

## Make it even better

- Schedule a **morning sort** every weekday.
- Add **templates** for the replies you send most.
- Track **response time** for a shared inbox.

## If something goes wrong

- **Important email got set aside:** Add the sender to your "always matters" list.
- **Drafts don't show up:** Check that Gmail is connected with permission to create drafts.
