## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: event organizers, nonprofits, community groups, creators and brands.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: We're bringing an event to a new city, I need sponsors, I'm looking for venue partners)
2. **What partner roles do you need?** Sponsors, venues, judges, creators, media
3. **What's the timeline?** When do you need partners by?
4. **What can partners get?** Logo placement, tickets, a speaking slot, posts
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Partner roles** with what each gives, gets and who to ask first
- **A partner page** on why you're doing this and how to get involved
- **Outreach emails** for each role, each with a subject line and a personal first line
- **A press release draft** that names partners once they sign, plus **open questions** to decide before sending

**Why it helps:** One generic pitch for every partner type falls flat. Each role needs its own reason to say yes.
