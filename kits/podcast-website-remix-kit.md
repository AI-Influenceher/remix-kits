## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: podcasters, video show hosts, creators and brands launching a show.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I'm starting an interview show, I want guests to apply, I need a simple show website)
2. **What's the show about, and who's it for?**
3. **What do you need to know before booking a guest?**
4. **Where will the site live?** Squarespace, WordPress, Wix, other
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

When you're done, you'll have:
