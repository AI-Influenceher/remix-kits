# Remix Kit: A Handoff Page for a New Leader

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Gather where things stand into one page with tabs, so a new leader or teammate can get up to speed and see their own list for the week.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | About 2 to 3 hours |
| **Skill level** | Some comfort with apps helps. No coding. |
| **You end up with** | A tabbed page with wins, open work, key dates, who does what and a weekly list for each person |

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: nonprofits hiring a new director, small businesses adding a partner, and teams handing off a role.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: We just hired a new director, I'm handing my role to someone, I'm bringing on a partner)
2. **Who's handing off, and who's taking over?** (Roles, not names, is fine)
3. **Where does the info live today?** Email, spreadsheets, notes, tools
4. **What tabs matter most?** Overview, projects, tasks, dates, money, who does what
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] Current numbers that matter (members, clients, renewals)
- [ ] Open projects and their status
- [ ] Key dates for the next 6 to 12 months
- [ ] A rough split of who owns what
- [ ] Recent wins worth knowing

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **tabbed page** with overview, wins, this week, projects, tasks, dates and more
- **A list for each leader** with due dates and links
- **Live task status** from a shared sheet

**Why it helps:** When facts are scattered across systems, a new person spends weeks just finding things.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Pick the tabs
Claude suggests tabs. Keep only what's useful.

### 5.2 Gather the facts
Connect tools or paste info. Claude only uses what it finds or you tell it.

### 5.3 Split the work
You and the other person decide who owns what. Claude records it.

### 5.4 Build the page
Claude builds it with a weekly list for each person.

### 5.5 Connect the sheet
Task status lives in a sheet so updates show on the page.

### 5.6 Review weekly
Open it first thing each week and update status.

## Make it even better

- Add a **90-day plan** tab.
- Add a **contacts** tab with who to call for what.
- Archive the page after 3 months as a record.

## If something goes wrong

- **Info is out of date:** Set a weekly reminder to refresh the numbers.
- **Too much on the page:** Hide tabs nobody opened in two weeks.
