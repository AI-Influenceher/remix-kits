## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **calendar view and a list view** of the same week, with a button to add a post
- Each post holding its **copy, image, date, time and team notes**
- A **clear status** on every post, so nobody guesses what's ready
- **Scheduling drafts** Claude builds from approved posts

**Why it helps:** Posts scattered across docs and chats make it hard to tell what's been reviewed. One page with a status per post fixes that.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Set your statuses
Pick the steps a post moves through. Four is a good number: Draft, Needs changes, In review, Approved.

### 5.2 Add your week
Share your post ideas, copy and images. Claude lays them out by day.

### 5.3 Build the page
Claude builds the planner with a calendar view, a list view and an add-post button, in your brand colors.

### 5.4 Review together
Share the page with your reviewer. They leave notes and change statuses.

### 5.5 Turn approved posts into drafts
Ask Claude to read the page and prepare scheduling drafts for every approved post, in your scheduling tool if it's connected.

### 5.6 Repeat weekly
Start each week by asking Claude to clear out last week and add the new posts.

## Make it even better

- Add a **caption checker** that flags posts over a platform's character limit.
- Add **hashtag and alt text fields** so accessibility is built in.
- Set a **scheduled task** that reminds reviewers on Thursday when posts are still in review.

## If something goes wrong

- **Notes disappeared:** Some pages save notes only in your own browser. Ask Claude to use shared saving so the whole team sees the same thing.
- **Drafts didn't show in my scheduler:** Check that the scheduling tool is connected to Claude, or ask for copy-ready drafts instead.
