## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: nonprofits with chapters, franchise owners, clubs, volunteer networks and companies with regional teams.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I run a network of local chapters, I coordinate regional team leads, I organize a club with city groups)
2. **How many groups or leaders are there?**
3. **How often do leaders meet?** Weekly, monthly, quarterly
4. **What do leaders ask for most?** Dates, numbers, templates, approvals, news
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] A list of your groups and their leaders
- [ ] The next few meeting dates and key dates for the year
- [ ] The numbers leaders care about (counts only, like members or events)
- [ ] Toolkit files: templates, logos, how-to guides
- [ ] Anything leaders can approve themselves, like new volunteers

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- An **agenda anyone can add to** before the call, with items ticked off and the rest rolling over
- **Two-minute updates** where each group posts a win, its next event and one ask
- **Numbers and dates** in one place
- A **toolkit** section and a spot for leaders to handle approvals for their own group

**Why it helps:** When leaders miss a call, news gets lost in email. A hub lets everyone catch up on their own time.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Map what leaders need
Claude lists the sections based on your answers. Cut anything leaders won't use.

### 5.2 Gather the content
Share groups, dates, numbers and toolkit links.

### 5.3 Build the hub
Claude builds the page with tabs or sections, in your brand colors.

### 5.4 Set up the agenda
Leaders can add an item before each call. Discussed items get ticked off, the rest carry over.

### 5.5 Share it
Send the link to leaders with a short note on how to post an update.

### 5.6 Keep it fresh
After each call, ask Claude to update the numbers and dates.

---

## Make it even better

- Connect a **Google Sheet** so leaders' updates feed the page automatically.
- Add a **new leader welcome** section with the first five things to do.
- Keep **money out** of shared pages unless every leader should see it.

## If something goes wrong

- **Leaders see each other's private info:** Move anything sensitive off the page or into a sign-in protected area.
- **Nobody adds agenda items:** Send a reminder two days before the call with the link.
