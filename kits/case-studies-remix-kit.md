## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: agencies, freelancers, consultants, contractors and any service business.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: My portfolio page is thin, I want to show results, I need proof for a new offer)
2. **Which offers should the case studies support?**
3. **Which clients can be named, and which stay anonymous?**
4. **What results do you have?** (Real numbers with time frames)
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Ready-to-paste blocks**: headline, label, two-sentence summary and stats
- **A time frame on every stat**, so no number reads as inflated
- **A page title and description** for search on each block
- **Approval notes**: who can be named, who stays anonymous and who's on hold

**Why it helps:** Proof sells. Stats without time frames look made up.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Pick the clients
Choose 3 to 6 that match your main offers.

### 5.2 Gather the facts
Results and time frames only.

### 5.3 Write the blocks
Claude drafts each one.

### 5.4 Check approvals
Mark each as named, anonymous or on hold.

### 5.5 Paste them in
Claude gives steps for your site builder.

## If something goes wrong

- **No numbers:** Use clear before-and-after descriptions instead.
- **Client said no to naming:** Describe them by type, like "a regional bakery."
