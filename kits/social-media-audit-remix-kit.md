**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Turn a year of social stats into findings someone can act on: what works, when to post, and ranked ideas for the next 90 days.

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Headline numbers** and the post that beat the rest
- **What works and when**: themes, formats, video length and best posting times, each with a chart
- A **scorecard** for events or campaigns on one scale
- **15 ideas ranked by impact and effort**, then given dates for 90 days

**Why it helps:** A pile of stats doesn't tell you what to do next. An audit does.

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Export the stats
Claude tells you where to find them.

### 5.2 Find the patterns
Themes, formats, timing.

### 5.3 Score the campaigns
One scale for every event or campaign.

### 5.4 Rank ideas
Claude lists ideas by impact and effort.

### 5.5 Build the plan
Ideas get dates over 90 days.

### 5.6 Make the report
A shareable page with charts.

## If something goes wrong

- **Charts look empty:** Check the export includes post-level data, not just totals.
- **Findings feel obvious:** Ask Claude for the three most surprising patterns.
