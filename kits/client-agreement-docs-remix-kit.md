## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: freelancers, agencies, consultants, contractors and creatives.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I just closed a new client, I'm sending my first contract, I want a reusable template)
2. **What did you agree on the call?** Services, timing, price
3. **Do you have a project timeline already?** (Dates and payments should match it)
4. **How will it be signed?** E-signature, PDF, or just email approval
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **scope of work** with included services, dates, handover steps, fees and what's not covered
- A **services agreement** with deliverables, deposits and due dates that line up with your timeline
- **Plain terms** a first-time client can follow

**Why it helps:** Clear documents prevent the "I thought that was included" conversation later.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Share your notes
Upload call notes or a transcript.

### 5.2 Draft the scope
Claude lists services, dates, fees and exclusions. Correct anything off.

### 5.3 Match the dates
If you have a timeline, Claude lines up every payment and date with it.

### 5.4 Draft the agreement
Claude writes plain terms for each side's responsibilities.

### 5.5 Review with a lawyer
Have a lawyer look before you use it.
