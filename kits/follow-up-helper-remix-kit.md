# Remix Kit: A Follow-Up Email Helper

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Build a reusable helper that drafts short, personal follow-up emails to leads and past clients, so good relationships don't go quiet.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | About 1 hour to set up, then minutes per email |
| **Skill level** | Beginner friendly. No coding. |
| **You end up with** | A saved Claude skill that drafts follow-ups in your voice and reminds you when to check in next |

---

## How to use this file

1. Download this file.
2. Open a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and attach it.
3. Type: **"Let's start this remix kit."**
4. Claude will walk you through it one step at a time. Answer its questions, then keep going.

You don't need to read the rest unless you're curious. Claude will handle it.

## Instructions for Claude (read this first)

You are guiding someone through building their own version of this project. Follow these rules for the whole conversation:

- Go one step at a time. Finish a step, confirm it with the person, then move to the next.
- Ask questions as short multiple-choice options whenever you can, so the person can tap instead of type.
- Assume the person may be new to tech. Explain any technical word in a few plain words the first time you use it.
- Use only what the person tells you or shows you. Never invent shared history, names, numbers or results in any email.
- Never send an email. Always save or show drafts for the person to review.
- At the end of each step, say in one line what's done and what's next.

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the emails sound like the person and fit their work. Anyone who follows up with people can use this kit: freelancers, agency owners, consultants, salespeople, nonprofit fundraisers, creators pitching brands.

1. **What do you do, and who do you follow up with?** (Leads, past clients, partners, sponsors, donors)
2. **What usually happens when follow-ups slip?** (Deals stall, people forget you, renewals get missed)
3. **How do you want to sound?** Warm and casual, friendly but professional, short and direct
4. **Words or habits to avoid?** (For example: no exclamation points, no "just checking in," no em dashes)
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 2: Your tech stack

A "tech stack" is just the list of tools a project uses.

| Tool | Needed? | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Yes | Writes the drafts and stores your rules as a skill |
| A list of who you follow up with | Yes | A spreadsheet, a notes doc, or a CRM (a contact list tool like [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com)) |
| Your email | Optional | If you connect [Gmail](https://mail.google.com) or Outlook to Claude, it can read past emails and save drafts for you |

**What's a skill?** A skill is a saved set of instructions Claude follows every time you ask for that kind of task. You set it up once, then just say "draft a follow-up to Jordan."

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] **Your follow-up list** with each person's name, where things stand and when you last talked
- [ ] **3 emails you wrote** that sound like you at your best. Claude uses these to learn your voice.
- [ ] **2 or 3 real wins** you're allowed to mention (results, projects, testimonials). Real ones only.
- [ ] **One easy next step** you like to offer, such as a booking link or a quick yes or no question
- [ ] **How often you want to check in** with each type of person (for example every 30 days for leads, every 90 for past clients)

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have a helper that:

- **Reads the history first**, so it never repeats or contradicts what was already said
- **Gives every email one goal**: set a call, close a deal, share a win or simply check in
- **Matches the tone to the situation**: a gentle nudge after a proposal, something useful for a cold lead, a warm note with no pitch for a past client
- **Keeps emails short** (about 120 words) with a subject line and one easy next step
- **Saves drafts only**, so nothing goes out without you
- **Offers to set the next reminder**, so the follow-up after this one doesn't slip either

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Set your follow-up types
Claude suggests 3 or 4 types of follow-up based on your answers (for example: proposal sent, cold lead, past client, asked me to check back later). You pick and rename them.

### 5.2 Teach it your voice
Share your 3 sample emails. Claude writes a short description of your voice and a list of things to avoid. You approve or edit it.

### 5.3 Add your proof points and next step
Give Claude your real wins and your favorite next step. These are the only results it will ever mention.

### 5.4 Write the rules
Claude writes the full instructions: read history first, one goal per email, match the tone, under 120 words, drafts only, never invent anything, offer a reminder.

### 5.5 Test on 3 real people
Pick 3 people from your list, one from each type. Claude drafts an email for each. Tell it what to change until all 3 sound right.

### 5.6 Save it as a skill
Claude turns the final instructions into a skill and shows you a card to save it. From now on, just say "draft a follow-up to [name]."

### 5.7 Set up reminders (optional)
If your task or calendar tool is connected, Claude can add a "check in again" reminder after each draft.

## Make it even better

- **Run it weekly.** Ask Claude to set a scheduled task every Monday that lists who is due for a follow-up and drafts each one.
- **Look things up first.** Ask the skill to check for news about the person's company and mention it, with a link.
- **Track replies.** Add a column to your list for "replied?" so you can see which emails work.

## If something goes wrong

- **It sounds like a robot:** share 2 more sample emails and ask Claude to tighten the voice description.
- **It mentioned something that didn't happen:** tell Claude, and add a rule naming that mistake so it won't repeat it.
- **It can't see my emails:** connect your email in Claude's settings, or paste the last email into the chat.
