## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: nonprofits, events, podcasts, creators, clubs and community groups.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: We run an annual event, I host a podcast, we're a nonprofit with local chapters)
2. **What can sponsors support?** The whole organization, an event, a local group, an episode
3. **Do you want prices on the page?** Yes, ranges only, or no prices
4. **How should brands reach out?** One email, a form, a booking link
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A page that **feels inviting** to a brand deciding whether to reach out
- **Clear ways to support** you, from big to small
- **One way to start a conversation**, so nobody gets stuck on pricing

**Why it helps:** A page full of rates can end the conversation before it starts.

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 List your options
Claude turns your answers into 2 to 4 sponsorship options.

### 5.2 Write the page
Claude drafts the headline, options and a short why-us.

### 5.3 Build it
Claude builds the layout in your colors, ready for your site builder.

### 5.4 Publish and test
Send the link to a friendly sponsor and ask if anything's unclear.

## Make it even better

- Add a **downloadable one-pager** for sponsors to share with their team.

## If something goes wrong

- **Brands keep asking for prices:** Add a "packages start at" line, or a short FAQ.
- **No one reaches out:** Make the contact button bigger and repeat it at the bottom.
