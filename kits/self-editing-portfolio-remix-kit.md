## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: creatives, agencies, social media managers, creators and freelancers.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I make social content for clients, I'm a freelance video editor, I run a creative studio)
2. **What kind of work will you show?** Posts, videos, designs, campaigns
3. **How should visitors filter it?** Client, industry, platform, format
4. **Who will add new work?** Just you, or a team
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **An Edit button** that opens a form. Paste a link and it fills in the platform and handle.
- **Cover images** you drop in that get resized for you
- **Filters and sorting** by client, industry, platform and format, or by views and newest
- **A stats strip** that totals views and posts

**Why it helps:** If adding new work needs a developer, the portfolio goes stale.
