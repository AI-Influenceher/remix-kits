Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: associations, nonprofits, clubs, gyms, course creators and paid communities.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: We have a members-only section, our member perks are hard to find, people who lapse get locked out)
2. **What's the site built on, and what membership tool?**
3. **What does membership promise?** Perks, discounts, content, events
4. **What happens when a membership lapses today?**
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **A review** of the members area against what membership promises, with fixes ranked
- **A member home** with a pass card, the featured perk and benefits in tiles
- **A lapsed member page** that's friendly and keeps their account saved
- **A login fix** so lapsed members can still sign in and renew

**Why it helps:** A members area that leaks data or hides perks hurts trust and renewals.
