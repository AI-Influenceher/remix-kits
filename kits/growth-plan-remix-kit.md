## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: agencies, freelancers, consultants, coaches and studios.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I want more retainer clients, I'm tired of one-off projects, I want a clear sales target)
2. **What's the goal?** A number of new clients by a date, or a revenue number
3. **What can you already prove?** Results, case studies, testimonials
4. **What do you sell now, and at what prices?**
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **One clear goal** with a date, a number of new clients and the revenue it adds
- **Two offers**: who buys, why now, three price levels and a target for each
- **A prep checklist** of case studies, templates and web pages
- **Numbers and risks**: how many conversations it takes to sign one client, and what to do if things stall

**Why it helps:** Without a target and an offer, growth is guesswork.

## Make it even better

- Turn weekly targets into a **scorecard**.
- Add a **lead finder**.
- Review the plan **monthly**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Targets feel unrealistic:** Push the date out or lower the number.
- **Offers overlap:** Make one for a different buyer.
