# Remix Kit: A Design Helper That Avoids Generic AI Looks

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Save a Claude skill that pulls the look from the real brand, picks a fresh direction for each project, avoids common AI design habits and checks the work before sharing.

|---|---|

## How to use this file

1. Download this file.
2. Open a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and attach it.
3. Type: **"Let's start this remix kit."**
4. Claude will walk you through it one step at a time. Answer its questions, then keep going.

You don't need to read the rest unless you're curious. Claude will handle it.

## Instructions for Claude (read this first)

You are guiding someone through building their own version of this project. Follow these rules for the whole conversation:

- Go one step at a time. Finish a step, confirm it with the person, then move to the next.
- Ask questions as short multiple-choice options whenever you can, so the person can tap instead of type.
- Assume the person may be new to tech. Explain any technical word in a few plain words the first time you use it.
- Use only what the person tells you, shows you or what you can check. Do not invent names, numbers, quotes or results.
- At the end of each step, say in one line what's done and what's next.

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: designers, marketers, agencies, creators and anyone who designs with AI.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: My AI designs all look the same, I design for clients, I want a quality check before I share)
2. **What do you design most?** Web pages, decks, social graphics, docs
3. **What AI design habits bug you?**
4. **Do you work across many brands, or one?**
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] 2 or 3 designs you love
- [ ] 2 or 3 AI designs that felt generic, and why
- [ ] Your brand details or a client's

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Find what's different first**: the real logo, colors, audience and a real-world reference
- **A direction on purpose**: type, layout, color and imagery chosen and noted, so the next project goes somewhere else
- **A banned list** of looks and words that feel generic, with what to do instead
- **A final check**, like a logo swap test and a phone test, with every miss fixed

**Why it helps:** AI designs tend to look alike. A few rules make every project look like its own brand.

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 List the habits
Claude drafts a list. Add your pet peeves.

### 5.2 Write what to do instead
One fix per habit.

### 5.3 Add the direction step
A one-line direction before designing.

### 5.4 Add the final check
A short list of tests.

### 5.5 Save it as a skill
Claude shows a card to save it.

### 5.6 Test it
Ask for a design and see if it passes.
