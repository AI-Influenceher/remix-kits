## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: coaches, creators, consultants, agencies, nonprofits and small businesses.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I made a free checklist, I want to grow my email list, I'm testing interest in a paid class)
2. **What's the free resource?**
3. **Which email tool do you use?** Kit, Mailchimp, Substack, other
4. **What should people do after?** Buy something, book a call, join a class, just stay subscribed
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Sign-up page words**: a headline, a short promise and a button label
- **A confirmation email** with the download link up top
- **A welcome series** of four emails, each with a tip, the last one testing interest in a paid offer
- **A launch checklist** of every link and asset, with deadlines

**Why it helps:** A free giveaway takes a page, several emails and a lot of links, all in the right order.
