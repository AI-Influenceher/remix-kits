# Remix Kit: A Brand System Volunteers Can Use

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Pick a look from a few directions, then build it into a brand system with simple rules, ready-made pieces and a plain-language brand book.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | About 2 to 4 hours |
| **Skill level** | Beginner friendly. No coding. |
| **You end up with** | A chosen look, logo files, ready-made pieces like posts, cards and certificates, and a brand book |

---

## How to use this file

1. Download this file.
2. Open a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and attach it.
3. Type: **"Let's start this remix kit."**
4. Claude will walk you through it one step at a time. Answer its questions, then keep going.

You don't need to read the rest unless you're curious. Claude will handle it.

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: nonprofits, award programs, events, clubs and small businesses where many people make materials.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: Volunteers make our posts, we're launching an award, our materials never match)
2. **What's the brand for?**
3. **Who makes materials?** Staff, volunteers, a designer
4. **What pieces do you need?** Posts, stories, emails, signs, badges, certificates
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **A few looks** drawn and narrowed to one
- **One simple rule** people remember, like which color means what
- **Ready-made pieces** with blanks like [Name] so anyone can fill them in
- **Logo files** and **a plain brand book** on how it should look and sound

**Why it helps:** When many people make materials, simple rules and templates keep everything matching.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Explore looks
Claude draws 3 to 5 directions.

### 5.2 Pick one
Mix the best parts if you like.

### 5.3 Set the rules
Colors, type and one memorable rule.

### 5.4 Build the pieces
Each with a preview.

### 5.5 Write the brand book
Plain words.

### 5.6 Hand it off
Share files and the book with your team.
