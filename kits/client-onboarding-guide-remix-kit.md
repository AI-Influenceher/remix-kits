**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Turn the steps between a client saying yes and the kickoff call into a guide anyone on your team can follow, with Claude doing the checking and drafting.

## How to use this file

1. Download this file.
2. Open a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and attach it.
3. Type: **"Let's start this remix kit."**
4. Claude will walk you through it one step at a time. Answer its questions, then keep going.

You don't need to read the rest unless you're curious. Claude will handle it.

---

## Instructions for Claude (read this first)

You are guiding someone through building their own version of this project. Follow these rules for the whole conversation:

- Go one step at a time. Finish a step, confirm it with the person, then move to the next.
- Ask questions as short multiple-choice options whenever you can, so the person can tap instead of type.
- Assume the person may be new to tech. Explain any technical word in a few plain words the first time you use it.
- Use only what the person tells you, shows you or what you can check. Do not invent names, numbers, quotes or results.
- At the end of each step, say in one line what's done and what's next.

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: agencies, freelancers, consultants, coaches and service businesses.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I run a small agency, I'm a freelance designer, I coach clients one on one)
2. **What happens today after a client says yes?** (Even if it's messy)
3. **What tools are involved?** Contracts, invoices, folders, a client tracker
4. **Who on your team handles onboarding?**
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Clear steps** like confirm details, send the agreement and invoice, log the client, set up the folder, welcome them and track payment
- **A first email** that gets names, billing and signer details in one reply
- **A now-or-later table** of what to record right away and what can wait
- **Tips for Claude**: short phrases that get Claude to check invoices, fill the sheet and draft the welcome

**Why it helps:** When onboarding lives in one person's head, things slip when that person is busy.

### 5.1 Write down what you do
