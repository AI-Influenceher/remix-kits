## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **One list** of every idea, grouped by month and tagged
- **Search and filters** by word or topic
- **A link back** to the call each idea came from
- **An export button** to copy the list into a spreadsheet

**Why it helps:** Good ideas get buried in call notes and never used.
