## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: business owners, freelancers, students, parents and anyone juggling many roles.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I juggle clients, sales and home, I'm a student with work and school, I run a business and volunteer)
2. **What are your life areas?** (For example: work, clients, health, home, side project)
3. **How many tasks do you want to pick per day?**
4. **How should it look?** Calm and minimal, bright and fun, or try a few layouts first
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Lanes** for each life area, with a bar showing where your attention is going
- **Pick three for today**, with a gentle note if you pick more
- **Quiet-lane nudges** when nothing's done in a lane for a few days
- **Carry-over** for yesterday's unfinished picks, and saving that Claude can read

**Why it helps:** One long to-do list hides which parts of life are getting attention and which have gone quiet.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Name your lanes
Pick 3 to 6 areas.

### 5.2 Try layouts
Claude shows a few layout ideas. Pick or mix.

### 5.3 Build the board
Claude builds it with your lanes, the daily pick and nudges.

### 5.4 Turn on saving
Ask Claude to save tasks to your account so they match on every device and Claude can read them.

### 5.5 Do a check-in
Ask Claude "check in on my day." It reads the board and helps you pick three.

## Make it even better

- Set a **scheduled check-in** each morning.
- Add a **weekly review** that shows what got done in each lane.
- Add **streaks** for habits.

## If something goes wrong

- **Tasks vanished:** They may have been saved only in that browser. Ask Claude to switch to account saving.
- **Too many nudges:** Raise the number of quiet days before a flag.
