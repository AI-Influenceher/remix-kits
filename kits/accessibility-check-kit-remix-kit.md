## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Yes or no checks** for captions, image descriptions, color contrast, hashtags, emoji and link text
- **Simple scoring** that turns answers into a score anyone understands
- **A report outline** from findings to fixes
- **A talk pitch** outline if you want to speak about it

**Why it helps:** Many posts leave people out without anyone meaning to. A checklist makes it easy to catch.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Build the checklist
Claude drafts 10 to 15 checks.

### 5.2 Set the scoring
Simple points per check.

### 5.3 Review the posts
Go through each check.

### 5.4 Write the report
Findings, then fixes in order.

### 5.5 Share it
As a page or PDF.
