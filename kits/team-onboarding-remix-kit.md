# Remix Kit: A Team Onboarding Helper

**From the AI Influenceher build log.** Turn your new-hire checklist into a saved Claude skill that writes the welcome doc, tracks access and turns onboarding notes into tasks.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | About 1 to 2 hours to set up |
| **Skill level** | Beginner friendly. No coding. |
| **You end up with** | A saved skill that runs the same onboarding steps for every new hire or returning team member |

---

## How to use this file

1. Download this file.
2. Open a new chat at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and attach it.
3. Type: **"Let's start this remix kit."**
4. Claude will walk you through it one step at a time. Answer its questions, then keep going.

You don't need to read the rest unless you're curious. Claude will handle it.

---

## Instructions for Claude (read this first)

You are guiding someone through building their own version of this project. Follow these rules for the whole conversation:

- Go one step at a time. Finish a step, confirm it with the person, then move to the next.
- Ask questions as short multiple-choice options whenever you can, so the person can tap instead of type.
- Assume the person may be new to tech. Explain any technical word in a few plain words the first time you use it.
- Use only what the person tells you, shows you or what you can check. Do not invent names, numbers, quotes or results.
- At the end of each step, say in one line what's done and what's next.

## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: small business owners, agency owners, nonprofit directors and team leads.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: I run a small team, I bring on contractors often, I manage volunteers)
2. **What does a new person need in week one?** (Logins, tools, people to meet, first tasks)
3. **Where do you track your team?** A spreadsheet, an HR tool, or nowhere yet
4. **Where do tasks live?** Asana, Trello, Notion, other
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

---

## Step 2: Your tech stack

A "tech stack" is just the list of tools a project uses.

| Tool | Needed? | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [Claude](https://claude.ai) | Yes | Runs the onboarding steps as a skill |
| [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com) | Yes | The welcome doc for each person |
| [Google Sheets](https://sheets.google.com) | Optional | Your team or hiring tracker |
| [Asana](https://asana.com), Trello or Notion | Optional | Where onboarding tasks go |

**What's a skill?** A saved set of instructions Claude follows every time. Say "onboard Jordan as a designer" and it runs your whole checklist.

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] Your current onboarding checklist, even if it's messy
- [ ] A list of tools and who gives access to each
- [ ] Your team values or a short "how we work" note
- [ ] A past welcome email or doc you liked

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **welcome doc** for each person, so nobody starts with a blank page
- An **access checklist** and an updated row in your team tracker
- **Onboarding call notes turned into tasks**, with nothing retyped

**Why it helps:** Rebuilding the same checklist for every hire wastes time and things get missed.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Write down your checklist
Share what you do today. Claude organizes it into before day one, day one and week one.

### 5.2 Design the welcome doc
Claude drafts a template with a hello, people to know, tools, first tasks and FAQs.

### 5.3 Connect your tracker
Point Claude to your team sheet so it can add or update a row.

### 5.4 Add the call-to-tasks step
After an onboarding call, paste the notes and Claude creates tasks.

### 5.5 Save it as a skill
Claude shows you a card to save it.

### 5.6 Test on a pretend hire
Run it for a made-up person and fix anything that's off.

## Make it even better

- Add **role-specific** sections, like a designer or a sales version.
- Schedule a **30-day check-in** reminder automatically.
- Add an **offboarding** version for when people leave.

## If something goes wrong

- **It skipped a step:** Add the step to the skill as a numbered rule.
- **It can't reach my tracker:** Connect Google Sheets in Claude's settings, or paste the info in.
