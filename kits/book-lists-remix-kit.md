## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- A **clean list** with audio and duplicate copies dropped
- **Themed lists** like business, history or fiction
- A **shelf view** where each spine is one list, sized by how many books it holds
- **Copy buttons** and **title and author search links** for each book

**Why it helps:** A long loan history is hard to browse. Lists make it shareable.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Export your history
Claude tells you how to export from your library or Goodreads.

### 5.2 Clean it up
Claude drops duplicates and audio, then shows you what's left.

### 5.3 Group into themes
Claude suggests themes. Move books around as you like.

### 5.4 Build the shelf
Claude builds the page with spines, search and copy buttons.

### 5.5 Add shop links
Links search by title and author, since library ID numbers often point to the wrong edition.

## Make it even better

- Add a **favorites** badge on books you'd recommend most.
- Add **short notes** on why each book made the list.
- Share a list as a **social post**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Shop links go to the wrong book:** Make sure links search by title and author, not by library ID.
- **Too many tiny lists:** Ask Claude to merge lists under 5 books.
