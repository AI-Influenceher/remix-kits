## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Light and dark themes**, with a darker one for the stage
- **Type sizes** for slides and handouts
- **A signature element**, like a stripe or shape, that repeats across every piece
- **Ready-made pieces** like buttons, badges and slide titles, plus notes on tone

**Why it helps:** When everything matches, you look prepared and memorable.
