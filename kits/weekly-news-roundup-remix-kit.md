## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **Top updates** with their own cards, then the rest **filterable** by category or platform
- **A do-this line** for each item, and a source link on every one
- **Unconfirmed items kept apart** from the facts
- **Post drafts** in a few styles, with credits, and an archive of past weeks

**Why it helps:** People don't have time to read every announcement. A roundup does it for them.
