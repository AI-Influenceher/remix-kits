## Step 1: Who you are

Claude asks these first, so the result fits the person using it. Anyone can use this kit: agencies, speakers, consultants, coaches and small businesses.

1. **What do you do, and who is this for?** (For example: My site looks dated, I'm moving to bigger packages, I need a booking page)
2. **Which pages?** Home, services, pricing, about, media, contact
3. **What should visitors do?** Book a call, buy, hire you to speak, sign up
4. **What's the site built on?**
5. **How comfortable are you with tech?** New to this, I get by, or very comfortable

## Step 3: Content you'll need

Gather these before you start. Claude will ask for them in Step 5.

- [ ] Your current site link
- [ ] Real photos of you, your work or events
- [ ] Your brand colors and fonts
- [ ] Your packages or prices
- [ ] A few sites you like

---

## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **New page designs**, with options side by side so you can compare
- **A pricing or services page** buyers can compare at a glance, with add-ons and an FAQ
- **Big headlines and real photos** in place of small text
- **Clear buttons** to book or buy, plus color options to try

**Why it helps:** Your site should show where your business is going, not where it was.
