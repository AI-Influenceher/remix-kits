## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **A board on your website** that saves every task in a Google Sheet
- **Normal sign-in**, so volunteers use the login they already have
- **Filters** by group or project
- **A claim button** for open tasks

**Why it helps:** Volunteers are more likely to help when tasks are where they already go.
