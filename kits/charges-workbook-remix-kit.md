## Step 4: What this builds

When you're done, you'll have:

- **One clean list** of every recurring charge across all statements
- **What moves where**: the card it's on now and the card it should be on
- **Flags** for charges that look misplaced, doubled or unused
- A **payment calendar** so nothing is missed during the move

**Why it helps:** Every bank's statement looks different, which makes it hard to see the full picture.

---

## Step 5: The build, step by step

### 5.1 Upload statements
Share your statements. Claude learns each bank's layout.

### 5.2 Build the list
Claude pulls every charge into one list and groups repeats.

### 5.3 Mark business or personal
Claude suggests a label for each. You confirm.

### 5.4 Review the flags
Check anything marked doubled or unused.

### 5.5 Plan the move
Claude builds a calendar of what bills when, so you can switch cards in order.

## Make it even better

- Add a **tax category** column for your bookkeeper.
- Repeat **every quarter** to catch new subscriptions.
- Total **savings** from cancelled charges.

## If something goes wrong

- **Some charges are missing:** The statement may be a scanned image. Try a CSV download from your bank.
- **Labels are wrong:** Correct a few, then ask Claude to apply the same rule to similar charges.
